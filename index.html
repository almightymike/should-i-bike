How to read this

A guide, not a guarantee.

Each window uses the strongest forecast wind and gust, highest rain chance, and total predicted rain during those hours. The rating is a simple personal planning rule. Conditions can differ on exposed roads and change quickly.

FavourableWind under 20 km/h, gusts under 35 km/h, rain chance under 30%, and rain under 0.4 mm.
Use cautionAbove a favourable limit, but below the avoid limits.
Avoid exposed routesWind 30+ km/h, gusts 50+ km/h, rain chance 60%+, rain 1.5+ mm, or forecast thunder.
